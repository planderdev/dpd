    CEO MESSAGE

    We improve production sites with practical automation solutions.

    Founded in May 2022, DPD builds on field experience in factory automation. We supply products through manufacturing partnerships and support customers beyond delivery with technical consultation and after-sales response.

    Hello.

    DPD Co., Ltd. was founded in May 2022 based on years of practical experience and technical expertise in factory automation.

    In line with the rapidly changing era of the Fourth Industrial Revolution, we continue to improve quality and service while contributing to industrial progress.

    DPD supplies a wide product portfolio through partnerships with various manufacturers. Recently, we have focused on cooperation with excellent factory automation component companies in China to provide Korean customers with stable access to products with strong performance and price competitiveness.

    We go beyond component delivery by taking responsibility for technical support and after-sales response so customers can use products more conveniently and reliably on site.

    From product selection to installation, operation, and maintenance, our goal is to provide total solutions that deliver practical value to customer sites.

    DPD will continue to provide components quickly and accurately according to customer needs, and grow as a future-leading company through creative and innovative thinking.

    Under the management philosophy of fair business, we pursue fair transactions, ethical competition, and a people-centered culture where customers, members, and society can grow together.

    We will keep moving forward with change and innovation, broad product development, and excellent service to realize customer satisfaction.

    Thank you for your continued interest and support as DPD grows.

    CEO, DPD Co., Ltd.
